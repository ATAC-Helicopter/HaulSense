Indicator calibration

Physical order: player 5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 1. Left sweeps 4 → 4+5; right sweeps 2 → 2+1. Enable “Swap left and right” if your hardware orientation differs. Center mirrors truck lights when indicators are off. During signals only the selected outer pair lights up.

Player LED commands are sent only when their mask changes; unrelated rumble and RGB updates do not restart the LED animation. To verify individual LEDs, stop the service and run haulsense --led-test. The tool lights each LED from left to right, then clears them.

What the SDK can tell us

Wiper telemetry is an on/off state, so its optional tactile cue uses a gentle synthetic rhythm. Brake texture is pedal/deceleration feedback; it does not claim to detect ABS. Compatible rumble is supported; USB audio haptics are not implemented.
